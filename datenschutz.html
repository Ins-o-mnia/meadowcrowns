We use double opt-in. After you sign up, you get an email with a confirmation link. You’re only on the list once you tap it. That way nobody can sign up someone else’s address.

We store:

The legal basis for sending the newsletter is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We keep the time and IP address so we can show that you gave your consent (Art. 7(1) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Giving your email address is voluntary; without it we cannot send you the newsletter. The newsletter is for people aged 16 and over.

We keep the record of your consent until the end of the third calendar year after you unsubscribe, in case we need to show that you consented.

We do not track whether you open our emails or click links in them.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, using the unsubscribe link in every email or by sending us a short message. We then delete your address. To make sure we never email you again after you unsubscribe, it may stay on a block list (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
